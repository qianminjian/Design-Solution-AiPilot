    REQ-001
    Minimum clear floor height for Grade A office spaces
    All Grade A office floors shall maintain a minimum clear height of 2.75m from finished floor level to the lowest point of the ceiling soffit, excluding beams, ductwork, and services within designated service zones. This applies to all tenant floors from Level 3 to Level 12.
